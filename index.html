Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Freestyle Cards respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Freestyle Cards does not require user accounts, registration, or email verification to be used.

App Configuration & Preference Data

To provide core gameplay features, we process non-personal game setup data:

  • Gameplay State: Game settings, custom configurations, deck layouts, and personalized game table presets are temporarily managed in-memory or securely via Firebase on a session level to sync your multiplayer table.
  • Local Preferences: Language settings and configurations are saved locally on your device or linked anonymously.

Third-Party Services

We work with trusted backend infrastructure providers to facilitate gameplay sync and secure premium features. These services do not receive personal registration data or emails from us:

  • Firebase: Used to manage in-memory temporary room states and keep multiplayer elements perfectly synchronized.
  • RevenueCat: Processes anonymous platform purchase receipts to securely unlock premium content and custom themes.

Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, or trade personal data. Non-personal game preferences are used strictly to run your active game rooms.

Security

We take all reasonable operational measures to protect game room environments, but no real-time internet synchronization protocol is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We recommend reviewing this page periodically to stay informed.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or the app, please reach out to us via our support page.
